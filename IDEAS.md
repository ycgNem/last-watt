# Ideas

Anything not on ROADMAP.md, one line each with why. Reviewed at each gate, not acted on between them.

From building the slice (2026-10-11):
- A map with more bends near the Hearth: only two towers can stand at a bend here, so every later tower is on a straight. More bends would make placement a richer decision (and change the §6 economy).
- Shed is weak on default tiers: shedding a tier-3 Lab never helps the wall, because tier 3 only gets leftovers (still true with Works first: shedding the Works tier is the only shed that frees watts for the wall). Verbs that matter mid-wave are tier changes and Overcharge; a Dark Bait or Reroute (Milestone 6) may be what pillar 3 actually needs.
- The bot as a "ghost" opponent or a hint: show where a competent player would have put the next Lamp after a lost run.

Left open by the first td-critic review (2026-10-11), for the owner:
- Floodlight as a cone: every enemy inside shares the beam, re-derived from light, not a bounce. Its 4 : 2 : 1 spill (1.5 tiles) almost never lands on spaced Moths, and per money it delivers less than a Lamp. Biggest open design question in the wall.
- Leaks drain the Hearth's kW (say 1 kW per light) instead of 20 lights: a leak weakens the grid itself (pillar 1) and drops the Kingdom Rush hearts.
- Cut or defer: the Beacon (no measurable effect in any run), Lamp tier 2 research (the Lab's only job now), the empty tier 4 "Spare" bar (cut in the fifth session, `DECISIONS.md`), and the far deposit (C, never built on).
- An energy-side reason to call waves early, if one is wanted: trade Lab time in the breather for bounty, say.
- How much Overcharge a run should allow: the bot fires about 55, so it may be the dominant verb now that Jars are free from the start; a cooldown or a rising price would show it.
- Money still piles up late in the run (the bot ends with a few hundred unspent); a late sink that is an energy decision (a second Hearth? bigger Jars?) would use it.
- Wave 1 has about 18 s of dead air while the first Moth walks 27 tiles: spawn nearer, or let it start further in.

Parked by the sixth and seventh reviews (2026-10-11):
- Re-tooling an Extractor in place (Deep bit ↔ Coker): rejected for now, since every other branch is chosen once; the panel names sell-and-rebuild instead.
- A longer or held breather before a wave's first grid threat (Snuffers, Shades), so the player can act on the telegraph without calling it early by accident.
- Graded leaks (a light lost per leak scales with how starved the wall was): tried, broke the Two Roads verbs bar (numbers in `DECISIONS.md`, sixth review).
